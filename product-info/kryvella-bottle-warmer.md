## Description (EN)
A portable, battery-powered baby bottle warmer from Kryvella in beige. A built-in 8000 mAh lithium-ion battery powers six temperature settings from 38 to 50 °C, shown on an LED display. The seller states it reaches 42 °C in about 10–20 minutes and keeps milk warm for 6–10 hours. It also has a soft night light for night-time feeds and charges by USB (5 V/9 V, 2 A, about 3 h). Always check the milk temperature before feeding.
