### Digital thermometer – technical data (manual, EN)
- Battery: 1.5 V button cell **LR41 or SR41**; battery life >1000 measurements; replaceable (a pen is used to push out the battery tray)
- Measurement range: 32 °C to 42.9 °C (89.6–109.2 °F); accuracy ±0.1 °C (±0.2 °F) for 37–39.0 °C
- Operating and storage: -20 °C to 55 °C; 15–95 % RH; 80–105 kPa
- °C/°F switchable; recalls the last reading; low-battery dot; "Err" error signal; switches off automatically after 10 minutes
- Methods: armpit or under the tongue (5 minutes; ignore beeps). Manual: "<37.8 °C – no fever detected; >37.8 °C – possible fever detected"
- Note from the manual: the thermometer is suitable for use at room temperatures between 25 °C and 40 °C. The manual prints this as "<25 °C (50 °F)", which looks like a typo for 10 °C (50 °F).

## Care (manufacturer, summarised from the EN manual)
- Thermometer: wipe with a cloth dipped in warm soapy water. No abrasive or antibacterial agents and no solvents. Never immerse it, and never put it in a dishwasher, steriliser or boiling water. Store clean and dry.
- Nasal aspirator: take it apart, wash in warm water with washing-up liquid, then boil for 10 minutes and dry.
- Finger toothbrush: clean in a steriliser or boiling water, then let it cool and dry.
- Brush and comb: warm water with a little washing-up liquid, rinse, dry.
- Nail clipper and scissors: wipe with a damp cloth and dry. The cutting edges can be wiped with alcohol.
- Keep all items in the case in a dry place.

## Safety warnings (manufacturer, EN manual – shortened; the full text including HR is in the manual)
- Keep out of reach of infants, children or pets. Contains items with sharp edges and small parts, which are a choking hazard if inhaled or swallowed.
- **Risk of swallowing batteries!** The thermometer contains a button battery. If swallowed, it can cause severe internal burns within 2 hours and can lead to death. The battery compartment cover must be replaced properly if removed.
- After rectal or armpit use, do not use the thermometer orally.
- Batteries may explode if exposed to high temperatures or fire. Never recharge the battery. Do not short-circuit, take apart or crush it. Do not use rechargeable batteries. Replace all batteries at the same time.
- Do not use within 30 cm of RF devices (mobile phone, baby monitor, DECT) or near strong magnetic fields.
- Do not drop it, bend the tip or bite the metal tip. Do not use it when the display shows the low-battery dot.
- Not for use by persons with reduced capabilities without supervision.
- "Temperature measurement is not a substitute for going to your doctor." Never leave your child alone.
- Nasal aspirator: never use it in the mouth or ears; never point the tip at the centre of the nostril; do not insert it too far.
- Nail care: do not cut nails too close to the skin.
- WEEE crossed-out bin symbol and battery symbol are printed in the manual.

## Compliance note (important)
A clinical thermometer is a **medical device with a measuring function** under Regulation (EU) 2017/745 (MDR). It normally needs a CE mark followed by a notified-body number, a UDI and an EU Declaration of Conformity. The manual cites EN ISO 80601-2-56 and ISO 13485 but shows no CE/notified-body number in the pages we read. **Before selling, check the thermometer and box for "CE xxxx" and ask Kikkaboo for the EU DoC and the MDR class.** If you cannot verify this, consider not shipping the set. The thermometer also falls under WEEE and the Batteries Regulation (button cell).
