**CONFLICT – street number:** the terms page and the 2025 manuals say **Vasil Levski 121**. The website contact page says **Vasil Levski 135, 4000 Plovdiv**, and the grooming-set manual cover says "Kika Group Ltd. 135, Vasil Levski Str. 4003, Plovdiv" (also a different postcode). The address on Kikkaboo's legal terms (121, 4000) is the most likely registered seat. **Before printing, confirm which address goes on the insert (ask Kikkaboo, or check the Bulgarian Commercial Register for UIC 203763896).**

How Kikkaboo labels its products: every manual we could read says "Produced for Kika Group Ltd. ... Made in China" (grooming set, milk powder dispenser, bottle warmer). Under GPSR, Kika Group is therefore the **manufacturer**, as the company that markets the product under its own name and trademark (Art. 3(8) Reg. (EU) 2023/988). Because it is based in the EU, no separate EU responsible person is needed.

## Country of origin – general conflict

Faire lists "Made in: Bulgaria" for every Kikkaboo item. Kikkaboo's own manuals for the **grooming set** and the **milk powder dispenser** say **"Made in China"**. The EAN prefix 380 only shows that the barcode was registered with GS1 Bulgaria; it says nothing about where the item was made. One Kikkaboo brand text (via a retailer) says "Part of our textile range is produced in our production base in Plovdiv, Bulgaria" (https://www.peekaboo.ke/products/kikkaboo-ethan-wi-fi-baby-video-camera-monitor-white-age-newborn-above). **Read origin from the physical packaging of each item. Do not use "Made in Bulgaria" from Faire unless the box says so.**
