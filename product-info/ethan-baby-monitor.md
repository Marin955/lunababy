## Description (EN)
The KikkaBoo Ethan is a Wi-Fi baby camera that streams 2K video (2304 x 1296) to your phone through the CloudEdge app. It has night vision up to 10 m, motion detection with push alerts, two-way talk, and recording to a microSD card of up to 128 GB. It connects to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi only (not 5 GHz), and up to 5 users can share access. The box contains the camera, base, power adapter, mounting screws and a manual.
