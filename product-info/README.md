## Croatian / EU labelling – practical checklist

LunaBaby buys from other EU sellers (and one non-EU Amazon seller) and sells to Croatian consumers, so it is a **distributor** under GPSR. A distributor must check, before making a product available, that the manufacturer's and (where needed) importer's identity is shown, that the product has a type/batch/serial number, and that instructions and warnings are in Croatian (GPSR Art. 12). If LunaBaby sells a product under its own name or modifies it (e.g. repackages it in a gift box in a way that affects safety or the labelling), it can itself become the manufacturer (GPSR Art. 13). **Keep the original packaging and labels, or reproduce their information on the insert.**

### A. Every product (GPSR)
Sources: Reg. (EU) 2023/988 – https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/988/oj ; Croatian implementing act NN 78/2025 – https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2025_05_78_1016.html ; Commission guidelines C/2025/6233 – https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/C/2025/6233/oj
- [ ] Manufacturer name, registered trade name/trademark, **postal address and electronic contact** on the product, packaging or accompanying document (Art. 9(6)).
- [ ] If the manufacturer is outside the EU: the **EU responsible person** (name, postal and electronic address), per Art. 16 GPSR and Art. 4 Reg. (EU) 2019/1020 (https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2019/1020/oj). Relevant here for **Kryvella**.
- [ ] Importer name and address, where there is an importer (Art. 11).
- [ ] Type, batch or serial number (Art. 9(5)).
- [ ] Instructions and safety information in **Croatian, Latin script**. Art. 4 of the Croatian Act NN 78/2025 defines "a language easily understood by consumers" (GPSR Arts. 9, 11, 19, 21, 22, 36) as **Croatian and the Latin script**.
- [ ] Online listing (GPSR Art. 19): manufacturer name and address (plus the EU responsible person if applicable), product identifier/picture, and warnings/safety information in Croatian.
- [ ] Croatian Consumer Protection Act NN 19/2022 (amended NN 59/2023, 59/2026): product information, including the name and seat of the manufacturer or EU importer, must be in Croatian and Latin script (Art. 6(2) in the 2022 text). Documents prepared by the manufacturer (instructions, warnings, guarantees) must be identical to the original and in Croatian (Art. 46(1)(6) in the 2022 text). https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2022_02_19_203.html ; amendment https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2026_06_59_728.html. *(Article numbers taken from the 2022 text; check the consolidated version.)*

### B. Toys – rubber ducks, My Teddy comforter (and the night light if classed as a toy)
Sources: Toy Safety Directive 2009/48/EC – https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2009/48/oj ; REACH Annex XVII entries 51/52 (phthalates) – https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2006/1907/oj. A new EU Toy Safety Regulation was adopted in 2025 and replaces the Directive after a transition period (check the number and application dates on EUR-Lex).
- [ ] **CE marking** on the toy, a label or the packaging.
- [ ] Manufacturer (and importer) name, trade name/trademark and contact address. Type/batch/serial number.
- [ ] Manufacturer holds an EU Declaration of Conformity and EN 71-1 (mechanical), EN 71-2 (flammability) and EN 71-3 (migration of elements) test evidence. Ask for copies or at least the DoC.
- [ ] Age warning where relevant: "Ne preporučuje se djeci mlađoj od 36 mjeseci" plus the specific hazard. It must NOT be used on toys clearly meant for under-3s (e.g. a 0+ comforter).
- [ ] Phthalates (DEHP, DBP, BBP, DIBP) ≤ 0.1 % in plasticised material – relevant to PVC ducks.
- [ ] Warnings in Croatian, preceded by "Upozorenje:".
- [ ] **Ducks:** if the packaging says "decoration / not a toy", do not market them as a baby bath toy.

### C. Textiles – hooded towel, muslin cloth, My Teddy comforter
Source: Reg. (EU) No 1007/2011 – https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2011/1007/oj
- [ ] Fibre composition by **% of weight, using the Annex I fibre names**, on a durable, legible label, in Croatian (Member States may require their official language – Art. 16(3)). Examples: "100 % pamuk"; "80 % poliester, 20 % poliamid" (Annex I lists "polyamide or nylon").
- [ ] Toys made of textile (comforter): the toy rules apply first, but the fibre composition label is still expected. Check the sewn-in label.
- [ ] Care symbols (ISO 3758) are voluntary in the EU but recommended. Copy them from the label.

### D. Food-contact articles – silicone bib (pocket), Soina bowl, spoon & fork, milk powder dispenser, bottle warmers
Sources: Reg. (EC) 1935/2004 – https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2004/1935/oj ; plastics Reg. (EU) 10/2011 – https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2011/10/oj ; GMP Reg. (EC) 2023/2006 – https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2006/2023/oj ; BPA ban Reg. (EU) 2024/3190 – https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/3190/oj
- [ ] "Za kontakt s hranom" wording or the glass-and-fork symbol, unless the item is obviously meant for food (Art. 15).
- [ ] Name/address of the manufacturer or seller in the EU, and traceability (batch).
- [ ] Special instructions for safe use in Croatian (dishwasher, steriliser, microwave, max temperature).
- [ ] **Plastics** (dispenser, PP): a written **Declaration of Compliance** under Reg. 10/2011 Art. 15 must be available through the supply chain. Ask Kikkaboo.
- [ ] **Silicone** (bowl, spoon/fork, bib): there is no specific EU measure. Ask for a DoC under 1935/2004 plus a migration test (e.g. German LFGB/BfR XV or the French/Italian national rules).
- [ ] Children's cutlery: EN 14372 (voluntary standard; useful as evidence). Drinking cups (not in the box now): EN 14350.

### E. Electrical and electronic equipment and batteries – night light, Ethan monitor, Kryvella warmer, grooming-set thermometer
Sources: WEEE Directive 2012/19/EU – https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2012/19/oj ; Batteries Reg. (EU) 2023/1542 – https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1542/oj ; RoHS 2011/65/EU – https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2011/65/oj ; LVD 2014/35/EU – https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2014/35/oj ; EMC 2014/30/EU – https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2014/30/oj ; RED 2014/53/EU – https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2014/53/oj ; RED cybersecurity Delegated Reg. (EU) 2022/30 – https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_del/2022/30/oj ; MDR (EU) 2017/745 – https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2017/745/oj
- [ ] **CE marking** and an EU Declaration of Conformity (LVD/EMC/RoHS; **RED** for the Wi-Fi camera, including the cybersecurity requirements that apply since 1 Aug 2025).
- [ ] **Crossed-out wheelie-bin symbol** (WEEE) on the product, or on the packaging if the product is too small. Battery marking: the crossed-out bin and, for Li-ion, chemistry/capacity labelling under Reg. 2023/1542 as it phases in.
- [ ] Croatian instructions and safety warnings, including the charging warnings for Li-ion batteries and the button-cell swallowing warning (grooming-set thermometer).
- [ ] **Extended producer responsibility in Croatia:** the business that first makes EEE, batteries and packaging available on the Croatian market (which may be LunaBaby, since it buys from DE/ES/NL/CN sellers) normally has to register and report with the Croatian Environmental Protection and Energy Efficiency Fund (FZOEU) and pay fees. A German WEEE number (e.g. MAGS: 32794741) does not cover Croatia. **Verify LunaBaby's obligation with FZOEU / a compliance adviser.** *(National instruments not verified in this research.)*
- [ ] **Clinical thermometer** (grooming set): a medical device under the MDR. Look for CE + notified-body number, UDI and the manufacturer's details on the device or packaging.

### F. Decorative article – wooden door sign
- [ ] GPSR as in section A (Croatian maker name and address, batch identifier, Croatian warnings).
- [ ] State clearly "Ukrasni predmet – nije igračka". If it has a hanging cord, add the strangulation warning and hang it out of reach.

### Suggested layout for the box insert (per product)
1. Name of product, brand, model/SKU/EAN, batch
2. Manufacturer: name, postal address, e-mail/web (+ EU responsible person / importer if any)
3. Materials / fibre composition (%), capacity, size, age
4. Instructions for use and care (HR)
5. Warnings – "UPOZORENJE:" (HR)
6. Disposal (WEEE/battery symbol text) where relevant
7. Seller: LunaBaby (name, address, contact)
