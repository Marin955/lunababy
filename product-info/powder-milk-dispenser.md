## Use and care (manufacturer leaflet, EN)
Before first use, take it apart, clean it, and place the parts in boiling water for 5 minutes. Wash after every use in warm water with a mild washing-up liquid and rinse thoroughly. Dishwasher safe, though hand washing is preferred because the dishwasher may wear the product faster. Steam-steriliser safe for 10–20 minutes. Do not microwave. Do not heat it in an oven, on hot plates or over open flames. Avoid sharp or abrasive materials, solvents and harsh chemicals. Dry thoroughly and store away from sunlight and heat. To prepare a feed: pour a portion of powder into a bottle of boiled water cooled to the right temperature, mix well, and always check the food temperature before feeding.

## Description (EN)
A three-compartment formula dispenser from KikkaBoo in blue, for carrying pre-measured portions of milk powder. The layers twist together to keep portions separate, and the funnel-shaped spout pours into most bottle necks. It is made of polypropylene and silicone, BPA-free, dishwasher safe and steam-steriliser safe. It is for dry powder only, not for liquids. Size 7.8 x 8.5 x 25 cm.
